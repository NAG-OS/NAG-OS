# NAG OS

### An India-focused mobile operating system experience

NAG OS is an independent mobile technology project being developed by **SAUBERKEIT SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED**.

The project explores a new mobile user experience combining a custom interface, privacy-focused features, integrated artificial intelligence, and a unified ecosystem designed with the Indian technology landscape in mind.

---

## 🚀 Vision

The vision of NAG OS is to create a distinctive mobile platform experience that gives users greater control over their device, privacy, digital interactions, and AI-powered assistance.

NAG OS is being developed as an evolving technology project, with multiple components working toward a unified mobile ecosystem.

---

## 🧩 NAG OS Ecosystem

### NAG Launcher

The primary user interface experience of NAG OS.

It focuses on a clean, intuitive and distinctive mobile interface designed around fast access to applications and system functions.

### NEEL BHAI AI

The AI assistant concept integrated into the NAG ecosystem.

NEEL BHAI is designed to provide natural voice-based interaction and AI assistance across the NAG OS experience.

### NAG HUB

A unified information and notification experience designed to bring important user notifications and information into one organized interface.

### NAG Privacy Shield

A privacy-focused concept designed to give users greater visibility and control over device privacy features such as camera, microphone and other sensitive access.

---

## 🔐 Privacy

Privacy is one of the core principles of the NAG OS project.

The project explores ways to make privacy controls more visible, understandable and accessible to everyday smartphone users.

---

## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence

NAG OS explores the integration of AI as a native part of the overall user experience rather than treating AI as a separate application.

The NEEL BHAI AI concept is intended to provide conversational and voice-based interaction within the NAG ecosystem.
